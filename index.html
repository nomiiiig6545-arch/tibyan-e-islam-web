Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Tibyan-e-Islam respects your privacy. This application is designed to be an Islamic utility and spiritual guide without tracking personal user data.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. All preferences (such as bookmarks, tasbeeh counters, and last read pages) are saved strictly locally on your own device.

2. Device Permissions

Location: Used strictly for computing accurate Prayer Timings and Qibla Direction based on your geographic coordinates. Your coordinates are kept only on your device, and we never receive or store them on any server of ours. To show your city name, the app may use your device's built-in location service (on many devices this is provided by Google), which may process your coordinates under its own privacy policy.

Notifications: Used strictly to notify you of prayer times and daily reflections.

Alarms & Reminders (Exact Alarms): Used so that prayer-time alarms and reminders you set ring at the precise time you choose, even if the device is idle. This permission only affects alarm timing and is never used to access any other data. When a prayer alarm rings, the app plays a short azan clip and shows a notification.

Background Audio: Quran recitation and the prayer alarm's azan clip are played through a background service so that audio continues when the screen is off. No audio or listening data is collected.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may fetch Quran audio streams and public Islamic datasets from open authentic APIs. No personal data is transmitted during these requests.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, you can contact the developer team at our official channels.
